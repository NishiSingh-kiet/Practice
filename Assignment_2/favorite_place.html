My Favorite Place - Ladhak

Ladhak is a beautiful place in India known for its stunning landscapes and rich culture.

About

Ladakh is a beautiful high-altitude region in northern India, known for its dramatic mountains, peaceful monasteries, clear blue lakes, and unique Tibetan-Buddhist culture.
Its main town, Leh, is a popular base for exploring the region. Famous attractions include Pangong Lake, Nubra Valley, Khardung La, Magnetic Hill, and Thiksey Monastery. Ladakh is also famous for adventure activities such as trekking, mountain biking, river rafting, and motorcycle trips.

Basic information 

Location: Northern India    
Area: 59,146 km²
Population: 274,289 (2011)
